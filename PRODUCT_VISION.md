# ALPHAC product direction

September 12 update: [LONG_TERM_GOALS.md](LONG_TERM_GOALS.md) records the latest combined-portfolio goals and explicitly includes API-key access and MCP servers after research and operational readiness. It supersedes older numeric aspirations.

Owner direction, 11 September 2026: build a complete API service that lets
people analyze the algorithms clearly, followed by high-quality MCP servers.
Transparency and understandable evidence are central to the product.

Current priority remains algorithm research and improvement. API/MCP design,
implementation and launch are deferred until the underlying algorithms and
reported evidence justify them. No launch date or performance guarantee is set.

Carry forward these requirements when product work begins:

- Explain each algorithm's economic hypothesis, inputs, constraints and version.
- Distinguish backtests, paper trading and live results, with periods and costs.
- Expose reproducible evaluation evidence, uncertainty, data limitations and
  failed/rejected research as well as successful results.
- Make portfolio contributions, dependence, drawdowns and capacity understandable.
- Offer documented, versioned analysis APIs and MCP tools with clear permissions.

These are analysis-service goals; they do not authorize publishing private data,
credentials, third-party licensed datasets or adding trade execution permissions.
