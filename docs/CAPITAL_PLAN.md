## 2. The raise — three phases, smallest-and-safest first

### Phase 1 (now → 3 months): family seed, as a managed account
- **Who:** your parents / closest circle.
- **How much:** a small seed — enough to make the record *real money* (decision: see §6).
- **Structure:** a **Separately Managed Account (SMA)** in their name — you trade it via
  limited trading authority / trade-only API keys. **NOT a pooled fund** (pooling triggers
  fund-regulation, audits, a prospectus — far too heavy for a first cheque). Fees handled via
  PayPal, per our setup.
- **Why:** converts the paper record into a **real-money live record** (the single biggest
  credibility upgrade) and seeds the flywheel.
  > ⚠️ **CORRECTION 2026-08-02:** this line previously read "without regulatory burden." That
  > was wrong and load-bearing. Managing a family member's account **for a fee** is potentially
  > licensable Managing-Assets activity (DFSA/FSRA/SCA); family status is not an automatic
  > exemption. Phase 1 requires regulatory-counsel sign-off before any outside money — including
  > a parent's — is accepted. Trading **your own** capital needs no licence and no entity, and
  > delivers the same real-money-record benefit, so it is the correct Phase 1.

### Phase 2 (6 → 12 months): first external SMA
- **Trigger:** ~6 months of clean real-money record + the forward Sharpe tracking the backtest.
- **Who:** one external SMA — a sophisticated individual / small family office (the buyer who
  *values* uncorrelation, not headline numbers).
- **How much:** $250k–$1M.
- **Structure:** still an SMA (their account, your authority). Standard-ish economics once there is
  a real record (e.g. 1–1.5% mgmt / 15–20% perf — set later, against a hurdle).

### Phase 3 (12 months+): a fund vehicle
- **Trigger:** AUM and demand justify the setup cost; a *proven B* grade.
- **Structure:** a Dubai vehicle (DIFC / ADGM fund, or an offshore feeder) — decided with a lawyer
  when the economics clear the ~$30–60k/yr setup-and-compliance cost. Not before.
