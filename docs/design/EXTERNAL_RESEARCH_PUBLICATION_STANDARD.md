## Purpose

Canli Capital pages are the living evidence interface. They are not the only preservation or
distribution venue. Every substantial sleeve paper must become a portable scholarly object that a
reader can cite, download, verify and reproduce without trusting the website.

External posting does not turn a preprint into peer review. Repository records must say `PREPRINT`,
`NOT PEER REVIEWED`, `RESEARCH SIMULATION` or `ALPACA PAPER` wherever applicable. Download counts,
DOIs and search rankings are distribution evidence, not validation of returns.

## Publication object per sleeve

Each release is one immutable versioned directory containing:

1. archival PDF/A-style paper with title, version, date, Arhan Canli byline, disclosures and stable
   citation identity;
2. LaTeX source and bibliography;
3. machine-readable `paper.json` with abstract, keywords, claims, failed claims, capital kind,
   evidence dates and related objects;
4. `CITATION.cff` and CodeMeta metadata with Arhan Canli as creator;
5. exact result JSON and all non-restricted tables used by the paper;
6. data manifest containing source, license, point-in-time field, timestamps, row counts and hashes;
7. code commit, environment lock, deterministic reproduction command and expected output hashes;
8. trial-family and complete-union accounting;
9. cost, execution, capacity, stress, uncertainty and diversification appendices;
10. broker-reconciliation evidence when the paper discusses Alpaca paper trading;
11. `SHA256SUMS`, SPDX software bill of materials, license files and a machine-readable RO-Crate
    inventory; and
12. a limitations and corrections ledger that survives every later version.

The paper and bundle are separate research objects. A paper DOI must not be reused for a dataset or
software archive, and an existing DOI must be supplied rather than minting a duplicate identifier.
Zenodo's official guidance explicitly warns against assigning a second DOI to the same object.

## Distribution routes

### Finance working paper: SSRN currently excluded

SSRN is not a current ALPHAC submission route. Its official guidance, updated 3 August 2026,
lists research written by high-school students and faculty among content types it typically does
not accept. The same guidance requires an AI disclosure in the abstract and PDF when AI was used,
warns that undisclosed substantial use may lead to rejection or account closure, and says rejected
submissions receive no individualized reason or appeal. No sleeve paper should be sent there while
the author is within the stated eligibility category. The route may be reconsidered only after
direct eligibility confirmation or a documented policy change. SSRN screening is not peer review.

- Current official submission guidance: <https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/get-started>

### Archival reproducibility object: Zenodo

Zenodo is the primary DOI archive for the distinct code/data/reproducibility bundle. Published
records receive DataCite DOIs; files become immutable and later changes use linked versions. A DOI
may be reserved before publication and embedded into the archive. GitHub releases can also be
archived through Zenodo once the repository is public and licensed.

- Record and immutability model: <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/about-records/>
- DOI rules: <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/describe-records/reserve-doi/>
- Versioning: <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/manage-versions/>
- GitHub DOI workflow: <https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/archiving-a-github-repository/referencing-and-citing-content>

### Optional disciplinary preprint: arXiv quantitative finance

A sleeve paper may be submitted to an appropriate quantitative-finance category only after it is a
real methodological contribution rather than a performance report and after current category,
format, moderation and endorsement requirements are satisfied. Acceptance is never assumed or
represented before the external record exists.

arXiv expects topical, refereeable scientific work, author self-submission, a distribution license
and moderation; a first submission or new category may require endorsement. The current 16 sleeve
papers are not queued for arXiv. A separate cross-sleeve methodology contribution must first be
authored, scope-checked and compiled under current arXiv format rules.

- Official submission guidance: <https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html>
- Official endorsement guidance: <https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html>
- Official moderation guidance: <https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html>

### OSF Preprints: excluded pending paper-specific review

OSF Preprints is not a default ALPHAC distribution route. Current OSF policy says that content
completely or mostly generated by LLM or AI tools is inappropriate and that organizations should
not use user accounts as publication aggregators, clearinghouses or DOI-minting services. ALPHAC
discloses reviewed AI-assisted development, so no OSF route may be reopened without a
paper-specific human-authorship audit, provider-specific scope review and owner action. This does
not assert that any paper is mostly AI-generated; that composition has not been independently
measured.

- Official OSF preprint guidance: <https://help.osf.io/article/376-preprints-home-page>
- Official moderation policy: <https://help.osf.io/article/691-preprint-moderation-policies>

## Release states

`SOURCE_ONLY` → `BUNDLE_INCOMPLETE` → `INTERNAL_READER_TESTED` → `EXTERNAL_REPLICATION_READY` →
`SUBMISSION_READY` → `SUBMITTED_PENDING` → `PUBLIC_PREPRINT` → `INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED`.

No automation may skip a state. `PUBLIC_PREPRINT` requires the external identifier and canonical
URL to be read back from the external platform. `INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED` requires a named
independent person or organization, their exact reproduction environment and their result, not an
internal rerun or another AI agent.

A checksum-bound source bundle does not by itself satisfy `EXTERNAL_REPLICATION_READY`. A bundle
may establish authorship, citation identity, source-paper binding, complete recorded-union trial
accounting, exact result-object hashes, code/environment bindings and replay commands while raw
input redistribution and an observed clean replay remain unresolved. Its manifest must retain
`BUNDLE_INCOMPLETE` until every requirement for the next state is actually evidenced. A mapped
command is not a completed replay. An isolated dependency replay in the same repository workspace
is stronger than a mapped command but does not satisfy portable clean-workspace reproduction.
Killed and null sleeves follow the same standard as positive sleeves.

## Quality gate before submission

- all visible numbers trace to a released artifact;
- the paper's result tables reproduce from a clean environment;
- all known negative and contradictory evidence is discussed;
- author, affiliation and contribution statements are accurate;
- no confidential credentials, broker identifiers or unlicensed vendor data are included;
- references resolve and distinguish peer-reviewed work from preprints;
- PDF text extraction, fonts, equations, links, bookmarks and accessibility pass;
- a fresh-context reader can identify the hypothesis, experiment, decision and limitations; and
- the external metadata exactly matches the paper and local canonical page.

## Human review ladder

The project records review as a sequence of distinct claims. Completing one level never implies
completion of a later level.

1. `AUTHOR_TECHNICAL_AUDIT`: Arhan reruns the analysis, traces every table and figure to an
   artifact, and answers the manuscript-specific authorship questions below.
2. `FRESH_CONTEXT_READER_REVIEW`: a human reader who did not prepare the manuscript identifies the
   hypothesis, estimand, test design, decision rule, result, limitations and reproduction command
   without verbal guidance from the author.
3. `EXTERNAL_DOMAIN_REVIEW`: at least two conflict-free external readers review the flagship
   manuscripts. At least one must be qualified to assess statistics, econometrics or quantitative
   finance. At least one must assess data lineage and reproducibility. A single reviewer may cover
   both roles only when their qualifications are recorded.
4. `INDEPENDENT_REPLICATION`: a person outside the project executes the released protocol in an
   environment they control and publishes the environment, commands, result hashes, deviations and
   outcome. Editorial comments alone do not satisfy this level.
5. `OPEN_PREPRINT_REVIEW`: a public review service records comments and author responses against an
   immutable manuscript version. PREreview can accept a request tied to a preprint DOI. Peer
   Community in Registered Reports is a candidate for future trials submitted before outcomes are
   known. Neither route may be claimed until its public record exists.
6. `FORMAL_PEER_REVIEW`: a journal or conference editor sends the manuscript to independent
   reviewers under that venue's policy. Submission, review in progress, revision, acceptance and
   publication are separate states.

The software paper follows the Journal of Open Source Software route only after the repository is
public, feature-complete, released under an Open Source Initiative approved license and supported
by more than six months of genuine public development history. It must also show demonstrated
research use, tests, documentation, open-source workflows and a contribution path. Journal of Open
Source Software review uses two or more reviewers in a public GitHub issue and assesses both the
paper and software. Current policy requires specific disclosure of AI-assisted work and human
verification. AI may not be used for author conversations with editors or reviewers except for
translation.

Every local review receipt records the manuscript hash, reviewer identity or venue-protected
identifier, relevant qualifications, conflicts, compensation, review scope, comments, author
responses, resulting commit and unresolved objections. A reviewer must not have a financial stake
in the reported strategy, be paid contingent on approval, or be represented by an AI agent.

The reviewer request and response formats are
`docs/design/EXTERNAL_REVIEWER_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md` and
`docs/design/EXTERNAL_REVIEW_RESPONSE_MATRIX_TEMPLATE.md`. Blank or internally completed
templates do not constitute external review.

The paper-specific recruitment, conflict-screening and commissioning sequence is
`docs/design/EXTERNAL_REVIEW_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md`. It distinguishes preservation, preprint
moderation, open commentary, independent replication and formal peer review. No route advances
until the corresponding external record exists.

The author begins with `docs/design/AUTHOR_TECHNICAL_AUDIT_TEMPLATE.md`. Its five research-account
answers must be written and approved by Arhan; automation may trace claims but may not invent those
answers or the approval.

- PREreview request workflow: <https://prereview.org/how-to-use>
- Peer Community in Registered Reports: <https://rr.peercommunityin.org/about/about>
- Journal of Open Source Software requirements: <https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html>

## Manuscript voice and authorship gate

Top-tier quantitative writing is specific, restrained and falsifiable. It does not depend on a
generic academic template or promotional language. Each manuscript must satisfy these rules before
external review:

- State the economic mechanism, estimand, decision rule and information set before interpreting
  performance.
- Separate measured facts, model estimates, assumptions and unresolved questions.
- Report negative evidence, search breadth, specification changes and contradictory results with
  the same precision as positive results.
- Prefer exact nouns and verbs. Do not use canned transitions, rhetorical questions, inflated
  adjectives or claims such as `revolutionary`, `groundbreaking` or `state of the art` without a
  defined comparison and evidence.
- Do not use em dashes in manuscript prose. Use a period, comma, colon or parenthetical sentence
  when it improves the argument.
- Do not use a fictitious collective voice. A sole-authored paper may use `I` for an authorial
  decision, while methods and results should usually identify the procedure or evidence directly.
- Vary structure only when the research question requires it. Do not manufacture stylistic
  variation to imitate a person.
- Keep technical notation where it removes ambiguity. Define symbols, units, horizons, universes
  and annualization conventions at first use.
- End with the actual decision and the evidence that could reverse it. Do not end with a generic
  summary or promotional forecast.

Before release, Arhan must provide manuscript-specific answers to five authorship questions:

1. Why was this question worth testing?
2. Which mechanism did you expect, and what evidence would falsify it?
3. Which implementation decision had the largest effect on the result?
4. What is the strongest evidence against your preferred interpretation?
5. What would you change in a prospective replication, without changing the reported historical
   decision?

The final manuscript must reflect those answers accurately. Passing an automated AI detector is
not a publication gate. Detector scores do not establish authorship, originality or research
quality. The project instead preserves Arhan's decision records, commits, experiment chronology,
corrections, signed approval and ability to defend every claim.

## AI assistance disclosure

AI tools are not authors or reviewers. Arhan remains responsible for every claim, citation,
calculation and submission. Each target venue receives the disclosure it currently requires,
including tool name, purpose, scope and human verification where applicable. The disclosure must
not imply that automated output supplied the scientific judgment or independently verified the
research.

The current Elsevier policy permits AI-assisted manuscript preparation with human oversight but
requires disclosure when assistance goes beyond basic spelling, grammar or punctuation. It also
requires authors to verify accuracy, originality and references. Venue policies must be checked
again immediately before submission because they can change.

- Elsevier generative AI policy for journals:
  <https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals>
