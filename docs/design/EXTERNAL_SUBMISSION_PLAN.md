## Repository-policy correction

The requirements were rechecked against official repository guidance on 2026-08-24. Zenodo remains the archival route for distinct paper and reproducibility objects; SSRN is excluded pending direct author-eligibility confirmation or a policy change; arXiv remains a possible route only for a separately authored, topical methodology paper. OSF Preprints is not a default route and is excluded pending a paper-specific human-authorship and provider-scope review.
