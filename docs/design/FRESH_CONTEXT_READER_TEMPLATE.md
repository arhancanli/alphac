A reader who did not prepare the manuscript tests whether the research can be understood without
verbal guidance from the author. This is a clarity and traceability review, not domain peer review
or independent replication.

## Questions answered without author guidance

1. What hypothesis and economic mechanism does the paper test?
2. What is the estimand, unit of observation, universe, and horizon?
3. What information was available at each decision time?
4. How does the procedure convert inputs into portfolio weights or a research decision?
5. What search breadth and decision rule govern the reported result?
6. What result did the paper observe, and what capital type produced it?
7. What is the strongest negative or contradictory evidence?
8. Which limitations matter most, and what evidence could reverse the conclusion?
9. Which exact command and evidence bundle should reproduce the result?

Record the manuscript section supporting every answer. Mark an answer `UNRESOLVED` when the paper
does not provide enough information. Do not infer missing facts from the website or a conversation
with the author.
