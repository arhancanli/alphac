## Venue decisions

### Zenodo

Zenodo remains the intended archival route for two genuinely distinct objects per sleeve: the
paper and the reproducibility bundle. Official guidance says resource type and license are
required, a DOI is registered on publication unless the object already has one, and published
files are immutable. The project therefore does not copy a local preparation date into the
publication-date field and does not invent or pre-claim a DOI.

- <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/about-records/>
- <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/describe-records/resource-type/>
- <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/describe-records/licenses/>
- <https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/describe-records/reserve-doi/>

### SSRN

SSRN is not a current target. Its official guidance, updated 3 August 2026, lists research written
by high-school students and faculty among content types it typically does not accept. It also
requires an AI disclosure with the abstract and in the PDF when AI was used, and states that
rejections receive no individualized reason or appeal. The route remains excluded unless direct
eligibility confirmation or a documented policy change removes this conflict.

- <https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/get-started>

### arXiv

None of the 16 sleeve papers is currently targeted to arXiv. A separate cross-sleeve methodology
paper may be evaluated only after it is authored as a topical, refereeable contribution and the
current registration, self-submission, license, format, moderation and possible endorsement
requirements are satisfied. Endorsement is not peer review.

- <https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html>
- <https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html>
- <https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html>

### OSF Preprints

OSF Preprints is excluded as a default route. Current OSF policy says content completely or mostly
generated by LLM or AI tools is inappropriate and warns organizations against using user accounts
as publication aggregators, clearinghouses or DOI-minting services. ALPHAC discloses reviewed
AI-assisted development. Reopening an OSF route therefore requires a paper-specific human-
authorship audit and provider-specific scope review. This report does not assert that any ALPHAC
paper is mostly AI-generated; that composition has not been independently measured.

- <https://help.osf.io/article/376-preprints-home-page>
- <https://help.osf.io/article/691-preprint-moderation-policies>
