# SEO audit: Canli Capital

Author and owner: Arhan Canli  
Observed: 2026-08-24  
Site: <https://canlicapital.com/>  
Status: technical crawl passes; strategic repairs prepared locally and not deployed

## Executive summary

Canli Capital is technically crawlable but not yet search-visible in the observed queries. Every
one of the 424 live sitemap pages passed the direct HTTP, canonical, metadata, H1 and JSON-LD
checks. The primary controllable problem is authority dilution: 226 incomplete trial-detail pages
are invited into the index, 1,369 canonical-corpus links navigate to raw Markdown, and 102 unique
raw Markdown targets lack a response-level noindex directive.

The corrected local build keeps the evidence public while concentrating index signals: 460 public
HTML pages, 234 sitemap URLs, 226 incomplete trial pages under `noindex, follow`, canonical paper
navigation, and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` for raw evidence. The full local publication gate passes.
No deployment, indexing improvement, traffic gain or ranking is claimed.

## On-page SEO

The live corpus has unique titles and descriptions, one H1 per page, self-canonicals and valid
structured data. The local repair adds four controls:

1. Incomplete trial pages remain public but use `noindex, follow` and leave the sitemap.
2. Complete evidenced trial kills remain indexable; packet completeness is not presented as
   strategy admission or future-return evidence.
3. Paper-to-paper navigation resolves to canonical HTML. Explicit source downloads remain
   available, with raw evidence covered by `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`.
4. The methodology copy is bound to admission v7: per-sleeve and book DSR are mandatory
   measurements, while 0.95 is the full-union portfolio-maturity threshold rather than a current
   per-sleeve or incremental-admission gate.

The two main hubs now use descriptive names: “Quantitative research methodology” and
“Quantitative research trial register.” This improves entity and intent clarity without adding a
new page.

## Keyword opportunities

The narrow opportunities are subjects where Canli Capital has original artifacts, not generic
investment terms:

- quantitative research trial accounting;
- deflated Sharpe ratio with complete trial-union accounting;
- point-in-time backtesting architecture and failure cases;
- paper-trading broker reconciliation;
- correlation-gated sleeve admission;
- execution-realistic backtesting and data-vintage controls; and
- publishing killed quantitative strategies and corrections.

Each intent should continue to resolve to one substantial canonical hub plus evidence, code and
limitations. New keyword-variant pages are rejected unless they answer a genuinely different
question.

## Competitor and SERP comparison

The observed DSR query surfaced general explainers, a Rust function reference and recent worked
examples. The point-in-time architecture query surfaced an open-source event-driven backtester.
The observed trial-accounting and broker-reconciliation queries did not return a clear exact-intent
authority in the collected result set. This is an opportunity, not proof that the queries have no
competition or that Canli Capital will rank.

Canli Capital's differentiator is not a longer explainer. It is the joined evidence chain: trial
identity, immutable first measurement, gate contract, correction history, machine source and
broker observation. Search copy should lead readers into that chain and state what it cannot prove.

## Prioritized recommendations

### High impact

1. Deploy the already-tested index-concentration and raw-evidence-header repair only through the
   authorized publication workflow.
2. Verify the domain and HTTPS prefix properties in Google Search Console, submit the sitemap and
   archive coverage measurements. This requires owner-controlled credentials.
3. Request inspection for `/`, `/founder`, `/research`, `/methodology`, `/trials`, `/performance`
   and `/verify`; record the actual exclusion reason for every failure.

### Medium impact

1. Publish external releases only when data rights, independent review and clean replay permit it.
2. Add real owner-controlled author profiles to `sameAs`; do not infer affiliations or credentials.
3. Earn citations through reproducible releases, corrections and novel measurement papers rather
   than purchased links or synthetic guest posts.

### Low impact until measured

1. Do not add pages solely to increase URL count.
2. Do not rewrite titles repeatedly without Search Console query evidence.
3. Do not claim SEO dominance, indexing or ranking from sitemap submission or IndexNow acceptance.
