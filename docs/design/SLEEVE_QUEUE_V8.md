## The point first

The raised ceiling gives room for about 75 more admissible tests. On 2026-09-23 there is nothing
to spend them on for free. The reachability screen finds 0 of its 20 untouched families testable
with what the repository holds: 13 need a vendor purchase, 2 need independent human labels, 1
needs an identity redesign, 1 needs history that is only now being collected, and 2 have marks
that are not executable. The families that were testable (earnings narrative change, the
AlphaTrend variants) are closed KILL or past the single-family tripwire of 40.

So the ceiling is not the binding constraint any more. The binding constraint is one of two owner
decisions: pay an independent reviewer, or buy data. Until one is made, no identity above 400
should be spent, because the only families left would be the ones already shown not to work.

## Order of unlocks

Ranked by how many families one decision opens and how strong the outside evidence for the
mechanism is. Costs are not in the repository; each line needs a quote before it is a decision.

1. **An independent reviewer** (human labels, no AI assistance, signed attestation). Opens
   `active_ownership_escalation` (48 labels; machine gates already pass) and
   `merger_arbitrage` (the sealed 2006 to 2015 confirmatory design; about 400 anchors).
   `tender_offer_spread` is the same mechanism as merger arbitrage's SC 14D9 stratum, so the two
   can yield at most one sleeve. Merger-arbitrage spreads are among the best documented premia in
   the literature; their risk is a crash beta in market sell-offs, which the book-level gates
   measure.
2. **A rates vendor** with publication rights for derived results. Opens
   `cross_currency_basis` and `swap_spread_dislocation`. Both are documented limits-to-arbitrage
   premia and are economically unlike every sleeve in the book, which is what the diversification
   gates reward. The Treasury leg is already held from 1962.
3. **Analyst estimate history** (I/B/E/S, FactSet or Visible Alpha). Opens
   `analyst_revision_drift`, one of the most replicated equity anomalies and also one of the most
   crowded; it would compete with the equity momentum sleeve for the same correlation budget.
4. **Securities-lending fees** (S&P Global Securities Finance or DataLend). Opens
   `securities_lending_supply`; the held short interest is a different quantity and cannot stand in.
5. **An index provider's historical constituents and eligibility rules**. Opens
   `index_reconstitution_flow` and, with rating histories, `fallen_angel_flow`; publication rights
   must be settled first.

The remaining vendor families (carbon, mortgage convexity, municipal basis, sovereign CDS against
FX, FX option risk reversals, credit against equity, inflation breakevens) open one family each
at a likely higher price and follow these five.

## Already running without an unlock

  2026-09-23 14:10Z (Bybit complete, Binance sampled). The contract needs three years of it.
- `dealer_gamma_pressure`: 2.5 years of the held expired-contract chain; the contract needs more.
- `spin_off_dislocation`: about 20 initial Form 10 registrations a year (204 in 2016 to 2025).
  Its next step is a registration-keyed protocol whose first question is whether that rate can
  meet the admission contract at all; it spends no identity.

## What would change this page

A reviewer engaged or a purchase made moves the matching family to the top, and the first
identity above 400 goes to it through a two-identity batch. At the 450 staged review the
queue is re-ranked on the governed results so far. At the 500 review the amendment's
pre-declared rule decides whether a step to 700 is even proposed.
