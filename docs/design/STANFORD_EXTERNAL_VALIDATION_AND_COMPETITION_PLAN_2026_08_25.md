## ISEF integrity contract

ISEF is the strongest scientific option only if the work remains Arhan's. Its official rules
require independent student research in the student's own words. The current AI table permits
some ideation, initial code, and statistical-tool identification under specified logging and
citation conditions. It forbids AI from initially writing the research plan, abstract, paper, or
poster; adding substantive points or conclusions; or generating citations. An affiliated fair may
adopt a stricter policy.

For an ISEF route:

1. Arhan chooses and writes a narrow question that is a substantive new contribution within the
   eligible research window.
2. Arhan and the adult sponsor complete required forms and obtain any required approval before
   experimentation.
3. The repository records which ideas, code, analysis, and prose Arhan produced and what mentors
   or tools contributed.
4. Competition materials are written by Arhan without generative-AI drafting. Existing project
   text may be used only when the fair confirms it is permitted and it is clearly prior work.
5. The display and abstract cover current-year work only. Previous work is labeled as background.

If these constraints cannot be met, ALPHAC should not enter ISEF. A clean non-entry is better than
an impressive-looking but ineligible submission.

## External-validation ladder

### Level 0: author control

Arhan completes each flagship technical-audit worksheet, reruns the analysis, explains the
mechanism and strongest contrary evidence, and signs the exact manuscript hash. Automated claim
tracing supports this audit but cannot answer the authorship questions for him.

### Level 1: clean reproduction

Each released packet runs in a fresh workspace from permitted inputs and reproduces its declared
result hashes. Source rights, secret scanning, restricted-data exclusion, environment locking, and
correction status must pass before public release.

### Level 2: independent criticism and replication

For each of the five flagship papers, recruit one methods reviewer and one data-lineage or
reproducibility reviewer through separate channels. Commission criticism, not endorsement. At
least one person outside the project then executes the release in an environment they control and
records commands, dependencies, deviations, hashes, and outcome.

### Level 3: immutable public scholarship

Publish approved paper and reproducibility objects as distinct Zenodo records only after rights,
license, metadata, author approval, and explicit account-action authorization are complete. Read
the DOI and canonical URL back from Zenodo before changing the local state to published. A Zenodo
record establishes preservation and citation identity, not peer review.

### Level 4: disciplinary distribution and open review

Evaluate one cross-sleeve methods paper for an appropriate arXiv quantitative-finance or computing
category. arXiv requires topical, refereeable work, moderation, self-submission, a distribution
license, and possibly endorsement. Endorsement is explicitly not peer review. After a supported
preprint has a DOI or accepted identifier, request public comments through PREreview. A request
does not guarantee that a review will occur.

### Level 5: formal software peer review

Prepare a Journal of Open Source Software submission only after ALPHAC has a feature-complete,
OSI-licensed public repository, documentation, tests, demonstrated research use, contribution
pathways, and more than six months of genuine public development history. JOSS reviews research
software, not new strategy-return claims. Its current policy also requires disclosure of
generative-AI use. Until those gates pass, JOSS is a milestone rather than a submission target.

## Publication waves

Wave 1 contains the five flagship manuscripts already named by the governed external-review
protocol. Each must complete author audit, paper-specific correction closure, source-rights
review, clean replay, fresh-context reading, two scoped external reviews, and one independent
replication attempt before it can be described as externally validated. An adverse review or
failed replication is still valuable evidence and must be published with the response.

Wave 2 contains the remaining eleven sleeve records. It starts only after the first Wave 1 object
has completed the workflow end to end. This prevents publication volume from overwhelming review
quality and gives the later papers a tested template without forcing identical prose or claims.

The cross-sleeve software or methodology paper is a separate object. It should explain the system's
claim graph, prospective gates, mutation tests, data lineage, correction model, and broker evidence.
It must not combine sixteen weak result stories into one promotional performance paper.
