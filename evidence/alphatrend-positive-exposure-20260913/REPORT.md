## Decision

Keep both confirmed long/short and positive-target curves as research controls. Do not deploy or invent a selective short rule from these exposures. Next reconcile saved instrument-level P&L, including fees, dividends and borrowing, over full and crisis periods before proposing a diversification-preserving change. SHY concentration and unresolved cash remuneration also limit interpretation of excess Sharpe; neither additional cash yield nor finance assumptions were inserted here.
