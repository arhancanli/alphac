# Crypto accounting coverage

The read-only audit of the latest retained local simulated state found **37 fills, 9 positions and 123.5443937264 quote units of recorded fill fees**. Replaying initial cash and those fills leaves **+935.6089310064 quote units of unexplained cash movement** relative to recorded cash. Position quantities reconstruct within the diagnostic tolerance. This is an unexplained residual, not identified funding, profit or a source of additional return.

The source stores fill fees in the state and fills table. `PaperBroker.apply_funding` directly updates cash, with idempotency delegated to callers; it does not append a payment identity, amount and receipt to the serialized state. `funding_health` explicitly records health rather than funding cashflows. Aggregate cash differences cannot establish the omitted event identities or completeness of funding, transfers, corrections or accruals. No historical payment was fabricated or backfilled.

Implemented `validation/crypto_accounting.py` and a read-only audit runner. Checks require explicit contract declarations for instrument identity, source reference, base quantity units, multiplier-one cash-plus-signed-inventory semantics and settlement/quote compatibility. Missing bindings, unsupported multipliers, mixed/unknown currency and absent USD conversion remain visible. Declarations are not authenticated by acceptance. Legacy cash replay uses existing float arithmetic and is diagnostic, not exact original accounting. Even a zero residual cannot clear missing event inventories.

The actual audit supplies no invented account or contract bindings: retained metadata is insufficient to certify them. The instrument model has a multiplier field, while inspected paper cash/funding arithmetic uses base quantity directly. Unsupported multipliers therefore block this assessment instead of silently rescaling the saved baseline. The ledger documentation declares USDT quote units; the report does not relabel them USD or assume parity.

**90 focused tests passed**, including 10 new accounting checks; Ruff passed. Coverage includes buy/sell cash and fees, closed positions, duplicates, malformed values, unexplained movements, non-unit multipliers, USDT conversion gaps and the inability of declared metadata to authenticate flow completeness. An independent Decimal calculation over the retained JSON verifies the residual to 1e-8 quote units. Decimal re-expression is a cross-check, not recovery of pre-float source precision. Saved state, source snapshots and hashes support reproduction.

The initial live-state attempt exposed a fixture mismatch: serialized fill sides are lowercase. The implementation and fixtures were corrected to the actual enum encoding before the successful run. Production is unchanged; this phase made no broker calls, new return trials, epoch changes or admissions. Valuation clearance and flow completeness remain false.
