A distinct investigation lead is cross-firm information propagation, rather than another own-price momentum window. Hou (2007), Industry Information Diffusion and the Lead-lag Effect in Stock Returns, reports predominantly within-industry large-to-small lead–lag effects. Only the primary abstract was reviewed; no full-method replication or modern profitability claim is made. Source: https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/20/4/1113/1615954
