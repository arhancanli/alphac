# Prospective settled-payment ledger

Implemented an isolated SQLite payment ledger using bounded decimal strings and fixed arithmetic precision. Initial cash is recorded separately. Each settled event and resulting cash balance commit in one transaction, keyed by source/event identity within an explicit account, epoch and currency. Exact replay is a no-op; conflicting replay blocks. Recovery checks event hashes, identities, intermediate balances and closing cash through a read-only connection.

Supported events are funding, fees, transfers and linked reversals. Funding requires declared instrument/contract, position and mark references, settlement schedule, quantity, rate and mark; the signed amount must match the multiplier-one equation. Zero funding is retained as an event. Reversals preserve the original event, require its opposite amount and reject a second reversal of the same target. Unknown events, missing references, mixed currency and unsupported contract multipliers reject.

**47 focused tests passed**, including 17 new ledger tests and 30 existing receipt/accounting tests. Real subprocess crashes before cash-update commit and after commit demonstrate all-or-nothing application and safe replay. Tests also cover positive/negative/zero funding, missing evidence, duplicate/conflicting identities, reversals, damaged balances/keys, missing databases and independence from caller Decimal precision. Ruff passed. A synthetic ledger and recovered balances accompany this packet.

This phase implements the settled-cash persistence core, not the entire acquisition specification. Source references are declarations rather than fetched/authenticated evidence; source-page cursors, interval manifests, settlement completeness, accrual/liability handling and binding to broker position state remain outstanding. Reversals can support corrections, but a replacement payment is a separate explicitly identified event. The account reference is local and does not prove broker ownership. Recovery verifies consistency, not protection against coordinated rewriting of the entire ledger.

No production integration, clock change, broker request, historical backfill or actual portfolio epoch was started. Synthetic fixture epochs are tests only. The existing 935.61 quote-unit residual remains unresolved at payment level. Flow completeness, source authentication, runtime and valuation clearance remain false. No new strategy trial or sleeve admission occurred.
