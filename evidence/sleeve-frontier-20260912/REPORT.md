## Refinery-margin hypothesis and first data sample

Research hypothesis, not a registered trading rule: unusually wide or narrow processing margins may induce production responses that predict subsequent margin normalization beyond outright energy trend and curve carry. Persistent capacity outages, product specifications, seasonality and location basis can invalidate this mechanism. CME describes crack spreads as a relationship between crude input and refined-product values, with supply responses affected by refinery profitability. Its examples establish mechanics, not profitable alpha. [CME mechanics](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/2024/trading-crack-spreads.html).

The standard long 3:2:1 recipe holds -3 WTI, +2 RBOB and +1 ULSD contracts. Crude is quoted in dollars/barrel, products in dollars/gallon; contract sizes are 1,000 barrels and 42,000 gallons respectively. A new exact-Decimal harness verifies recipe marks and signed mark changes. Nine tests pass, including zero spread, negative crude prices, each leg's multiplier and invalid inputs. It deliberately does not compute percentage returns on a spread that can cross zero. This is arithmetic, not a trading or capital model. [CME contract mechanics](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/2024/trading-crack-spreads.html).

Metadata estimates for six fixed requests totaled $0.16951821, under a $1 estimate cap. Actual billing is unverified. Acquired three days of definitions and three ten-minute quote samples for CL/RB/HO: January 15, 2015; April 20, 2020; July 15, 2025. These dates were fixed before downloads to cover older history, a known market stress date and recent history. There were 26,398 definition records and 935,435 quote records. Credentials were not written to artifacts. Metadata requests are free under Databento's documentation; downloaded data are billed separately. [Databento historical API](https://databento.com/docs/reference-historical/basics/).

The parent-symbol requests include exchange-listed spread instruments. After filtering to prior-known outright definitions, 253,724 quote rows remain; 251,376 have finite, noncrossed, two-sided prices and nonzero sizes. All samples have outright observations for all three products and common raw month/year suffixes. No sample has missing prior definition mappings or definition changes during its ten-minute window. Sampled contract units agree with the standard recipe. This passes only to contract-month and synchronized-quote auditing; matching suffixes and quote counts do not prove simultaneous tradability, depth, correct dollar normalization or historical specification continuity.

Before returns: resolve absolute delivery months and expiry/first-notice constraints; validate normalized price units against raw definitions; align three-leg executable quotes with fixed staleness limits; bind full-history coverage, rolls, capital/margin, exchange fees, spread/legging costs and outages. Then define one training/holdout protocol and reserve the identity before fitting or measuring forecasts. No signal sign, threshold, holding horizon or optimum month was selected from these samples.
